## Academic Rules and Grading

**Q: How are internal marks calculated?**
A: For standard theory subjects, there is an End Semester Exam (ESE) out of 60 marks and internal evaluation out of 40 marks. Internals are based on Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT exams), assignments, tutorials, and attendance. For integrated courses (which include both lab and theory), the ESE is conducted for 40 marks and internals are out of 60 marks.

**Q: What is the minimum attendance required to write the End Semester Exam?**
A: A minimum of 75% overall attendance is required in a course to be eligible to write the End Semester Examination (ESE). Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation on medical grounds.

**Q: When are the CAT exams usually conducted?**
A: Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT) are typically conducted twice per semester. CAT-1 is usually held midway through the semester, and CAT-2 is held towards the end of the classes before the ESE.

## Honours and Minors Programs

**Q: Will minors or honours be dropped if I have a supply or CGPA below 8.5?**
A: Yes, at the time of graduation, if a student has any pending supplementary exams (supplies) or if their CGPA falls below 8.5, their registered minors and/or honours program will be automatically dropped.

**Q: How many extra credits do I earn for completing a Minor or Honours?**
A: Successfully completing a Minor or Honours program awards the student an additional 20 credits beyond their standard B.Tech degree requirements.

**Q: Can I register for both a Minor and an Honours degree at the same time?**
A: Yes, a student is permitted to opt for both a Minor degree or an Honours degree.

**Q: What happens if I fail a course that is part of my Minor or Honours track?**
A: If you fail a Minor or Honours course, your registration for the Minor/Honours program will be cancelled. However, any Minor/Honours courses you have already passed will remain on your transcript and be counted as extra credits in your final grade card.

## General Information

**Q: What is the duration of the B.Tech CSE program?**
A: The B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering is a 4-year undergraduate degree program, which is divided into 8 semesters.

**Q: Where can I find the syllabus for my courses?**
A: The curriculum and syllabus for all courses can be accessed through the MBCET academic portal or the official department webpage under the 'Academics' or 'Syllabus' section.
