| Kerala Catholic Engineering College Managements' Association |
|  |
| PREFACE The Kerala Catholic Engineering College Managements' Association (KCECMA) represents 14 Engineering Colleges in Kerala. From 2007 onwards the Colleges were making admissions, purely on the basis of merit. Merit was being determined by combining the marks obtained by the candidates in the CET conducted by the Commissioner for Entrance Exams, and also the marks obtained by them for the relevant subjects in their qualifying exams. All students were to pay a uniform fees and for BPL candidates, scholarships were being provided. The state Government had signed a seat sharing agreement with these Colleges, whereby 50% of the seats would be permitted to be set apart to the CEE for allotment. All the students would pay the same fee. The Colleges would set apart a sum of Rs.3 lakhs per batch of 60, to be provided as scholarships to the BPL and other poor candidates. If any seats remain vacant after the last date for the Commissioner to make allotments, those seats would be reverted back to “open merit” category and would be filled by the Colleges. All the colleges are affiliated to the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Thiruvananthapuram. We hope to commence classes, as AICTE Directions. We solicit the co-operation of all concerned. |
