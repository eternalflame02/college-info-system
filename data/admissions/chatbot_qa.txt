🎓 **Eligibility & Qualifications**

*For B.Tech:*
- "What are the academic eligibility criteria for B.Tech admission?"
  - Answer: Candidates must have passed the Higher Secondary Examination of Kerala or an equivalent examination with at least 45% marks in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry/equivalent subjects put together.
- "What is the minimum percentage required in Plus Two (12th grade) for B.Tech?"
  - Answer: 45% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).
- "Is there any age limit for B.Tech admissions?"
  - Answer: Yes, applicants should have completed 17 years of age on 31st December 2025. No relaxation of age will be allowed.
- "Are both KEAM and JEE scores accepted for B.Tech admission?"
  - Answer: Yes, candidates should qualify in either the Engineering Entrance Exam KEAM 2025 conducted by CEE Govt. of Kerala or JEE 2025.
- "I belong to the SC/ST category. Do I have any relaxation in the qualifying marks?"
  - Answer: Yes, candidates eligible for SC/ST category relaxation need only a "Pass" in the qualifying examination (for Plus Two subjects).
- "Is the Eligibility Certificate mandatory if I studied in the CBSE board?"
  - Answer: No, an Eligibility Certificate is not required for students from Kerala HSE, VHSE, CBSE, and ISC boards.

*For M.Tech / Ph.D.:*
- "What is the minimum B.Tech aggregate percentage required to apply for M.Tech?"
  - Answer: A minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the qualifying B.Tech Degree Examination. For SEBC & OEC candidates, it is 55%. For SC/ST candidates, a pass is sufficient.
- "Can I apply for M.Tech if I am in my final semester of B.Tech?"
  - Answer: Yes, you can apply provided you have passed all subjects up to and including the 6th semester. Final admission confirmation requires the production of the qualifying degree.
- "Is a valid GATE score compulsory for M.Tech admission?"
  - Answer: While not strictly compulsory for all, a valid GATE score is highly preferred and is required to be eligible for AICTE Scholarships. Candidates with AMIE/AMIETE must have a valid GATE score.
- "Which M.Tech streams can I apply to if my B.Tech is in Electronics and Communication?"
  - Answer: You are eligible to apply for the M.Tech stream in IoT and Sensor Systems (ISS).
- "What is the procedure for Ph.D. admission?"
  - Answer: Candidates must qualify the Kerala Technological University (KTU) Ph.D. entrance test. The admission process is conducted exclusively through the KTU web portal.
- "Who are the approved research supervisors for Civil Engineering?"
  - Answer: The approved research supervisors for Civil Engineering are Dr. M. Satyakumar and Dr. Neethu Roy.

💰 **Fees, Scholarships, & Financial Aid**
- "What is the total first-year fee for a B.Tech Management Merit seat?"
  - Answer: The total first-year fee to be paid is Rs. 2,23,370/-, which includes an annual tuition fee of Rs. 1,00,500/-, a refundable deposit of Rs. 1,00,000/-, caution deposit of Rs. 10,000/-, and other university/facility fees.
- "How much is the interest-free refundable deposit, and when do I get it back?"
  - Answer: The interest-free refundable deposit is Rs. 1,00,000/-. It is a one-time payment made at the time of admission and is refundable after course completion.
- "What is the fee structure for NRI candidates?"
  - Answer: For NRI candidates, the total first-year fee is Rs. 1,23,370/-. They are exempted from the Rs. 1,00,000/- refundable deposit.
- "Are there any fee concessions for SC/ST or OEC category students?"
  - Answer: Yes, SC, ST & OEC category students need to pay Rs. 1,23,370/- at the time of admission, and the government will refund the eligible amount to their Bank Account.
- "What is the tuition fee per semester for the M.Tech program?"
  - Answer: The tuition fee per semester for the M.Tech program is Rs. 7,000/-.
- "Does the college provide any scholarships for B.Tech students?"
  - Answer: Yes, MBCET Scholarships 2025 are available. Details regarding the specific scholarship schemes are available on the College website.
- "Are AICTE scholarships available for M.Tech students with a GATE score?"
  - Answer: Yes, AICTE Scholarships are available to M.Tech students with a valid GATE score as per the prevailing Government of India norms.
- "What are the bank account details for remitting the college fees?"
  - Answer: Fees can be remitted to M/s Mar Baselios College of Engineering & Technology, The South Indian Bank Ltd. (Nalanchira Branch), A/c No: 0483053000009760, IFSC: SIBL0000483.

📊 **Quotas, Seat Allotment & Branches**
- "What B.Tech branches are offered at MBCET?"
  - Answer: B.Tech branches include Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering.
- "Are there specialized B.Tech programs like Artificial Intelligence?"
  - Answer: Yes, the college offers B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) and B.Tech in Electrical and Computer Engineering.
- "What is the total intake for Computer Science and Engineering?"
  - Answer: The total intake for the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering program is 120 seats.
- "How many seats are reserved under the Management quota vs. Government merit?"
  - Answer: Generally, 50% of the seats are Government Merit (allotted by CEE) and 50% are Management Quota (which includes 15% NRI seats).
- "Is there a community quota for Christians? How do I apply for it?"
  - Answer: Yes, candidates belonging to all denominations in the Christian Community are eligible to be considered in the community quota seats under the Management Quota. You must produce a Community Certificate from your Parish Priest.
- "What is the procedure for NRI admission? Who should I contact?"
  - Answer: For NRI Admission, candidates must fill out the Management-NRI Application form and provide documents like the Sponsor's passport, visa, NRI affidavit, and relationship certificate. You can contact +91 9447211341 for B.Tech NRI admissions.
- "Are there spot admissions available if seats are vacant?"
  - Answer: Yes, spot admissions are conducted for vacant seats based on merit. Candidates must report at the College Office with original documents.

📄 **Documents Required**
- "What documents do I need to bring for physical verification for B.Tech admission?"
  - Answer: Key documents include the Print out of the Application Form, 10th and 12th original mark lists/certificates, KEAM/JEE Admit Card and Data Sheet, original Transfer Certificate, Conduct Certificate, Medical Fitness Certificate, Passport size photos, and Fee receipt.
- "Do I need to submit my original Transfer Certificate (TC) at the time of admission?"
  - Answer: Yes, the original Transfer Certificate is compulsory at the time of admission.
- "What is the format for the Medical Fitness Certificate, and who should sign it?"
  - Answer: The format is available on the college website (mbcet.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/physical-fitness.pdf). It must be obtained from a Medical Officer in service not below the rank of an Assistant Surgeon.
- "Is a Migration Certificate required if I studied in the Kerala State Board?"
  - Answer: Yes, a Migration Certificate must be produced at the time of admission.
- "What extra documents are needed for NRI quota admissions?"
  - Answer: NRI candidates must provide an NRI Affidavit, a relation certificate with the sponsor, a copy of the sponsor's passport, and a copy of the sponsor's visa.
